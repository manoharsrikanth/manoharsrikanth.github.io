# Claim Verification Sheet — iPhone Duo & iPhone 18 Pro Max camera claims
**Site:** manoharsrikanth.com (source: `~/workspace/site-port`)
**Prepared:** October 3, 2026 · Review only — nothing on the live site was changed.
**Method:** Every factual claim about the two phones was extracted from index.html, story.html, inventions.html, kids/index.html, and kids/inventions.html, then checked against Google Patents records, the iFixit teardown, Apple's launch reporting, and reputable tech press.

## Flags — in priority order

1. **"Shipped in" linkage (inventions.html card; index.html timeline & lede).** The site states his patented architecture *shipped in* the Duo's hidden FaceTime camera and the 18 Pro Max's under-display Face ID camera as fact. Patent inventorship is verified and the technical correspondence is close, but **no public source (Apple, iFixit, press) confirms Apple's shipping implementation derives from or uses his patent**. Apple has never credited him publicly. Recommend softening to "the architecture his patents describe — consistent with what the teardown found."
2. **"I invented the disappearing camera." (index.html H1).** Defensible as first-person inventor voice — he *is* first-listed inventor on the foundational patent — but it sits next to Apple-assigned, co-invented patents, and commercial under-display cameras predate it (ZTE Axon 20, Sept 2020; Samsung Z Fold series). Keep it first-person; avoid any reading of sole credit for the shipping products.
3. **Duo ship date.** "It shipped in September 2026" is true for the 18 Pro Max (on sale Sept 18, 2026) but the Duo went on sale **October 23, 2026** (pre-orders Oct 16). Qualify the hero/timeline wording.
4. **Wrong product name (index.html line 279).** Hero video caption says "iPhone Fold's inner display" — the product is **iPhone Duo** ("Fold" was the rumor name). Fix.
5. **"Reduced notch" (index.html beauty lede).** The 18 Pro Max has a Dynamic Island, not a notch; press/Apple terminology is "smaller Dynamic Island." Adjust.
6. **Meta description** says the 18 Pro Max "moved Face ID under the glass" — only the IR camera moved. The page body carries the precision line, so this is low-risk, but tighten if convenient.
7. **2015 notes/prototype and 2016 demo** are personal account, correctly voiced in first person throughout. No independent verification is possible; keep as-is and do not present as externally corroborated.
8. **Citation ledger (27 publications / 13 assignees).** Ballpark-verified via Google Patents (Cited By 11 on US 11,294,422 + 21 on a family member, overlapping); the site's enumerated list is internally consistent (adds to 27; 21 US + 6 international). Exact split was not independently re-counted. The existing hedge ("prior-art references, not licenses or endorsements") should stay.

## Notes on verdict meanings

- **Verified**: confirmed against an independent public source (Google Patents record, iFixit teardown, Apple launch reporting, reputable press).
- **Partially verified**: the checkable parts confirm, but part of the claim rests on inference or is imprecise.
- **Personal account**: Manohar's own chronology/role as told in first person. Correctly voiced; no independent corroboration exists or is expected. Do not rephrase into third-person fact.
- **Needs softening**: the claim overstates or misstates what public sources confirm; a small wording change resolves it.
