Manohar Srikanth The Kids’ Corner Inventions explained like you’re ten

For curious minds · ages 10 and up

14 inventions (a.k.a. superpowers).

Every inventor collects ideas. Here are 14 of Manohar’s — each one a real patent, explained like you’re ten. A patent is how inventors call dibs: you write down exactly how your invention works, and the world agrees the idea was yours.

The collection

Pick a superpower.

US 11,294,422 · real patent

The Invisible Camera

What it does: Lets a camera take pictures through the phone screen — no hole, no notch, just glass.
Where you’d meet it: iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro Max.

US 2018/0082482 · real patent

Eyes In and Out

What it does: A headset display with sensors that watch the room and watch you — at the same time.
Where you’d meet it: AR and VR headsets.

US 11,217,021 · real patent

The Screen That Sees

What it does: Builds cameras and sensors right into the screen itself, so the display becomes an input too.
Where you’d meet it: Future phones.

US 10,817,594 · real patent

Your Wrist Is the Password

What it does: A watch camera that recognizes you by the pattern of veins in your wrist — no touching anything.
Where you’d meet it: Future smartwatches.

US 10,657,957 · real patent

Photos You Can Refocus

What it does: Records not just light but the direction light is traveling — so you can change the focus after taking the picture.
Where you’d meet it: Special light-field cameras.

US 11,550,408 · real patent

The Color Detective

What it does: A pen that points at anything — a leaf, a shirt — and captures its exact color.
Where you’d meet it: Drawing styluses.

WO 2024/238704 · real patent

Building the Headset

What it does: Designs for wearing an entire display on your face.
Where you’d meet it: Vision Pro and friends.

US 2025/0181155 · real patent

Eyes Without a Camera

What it does: Figures out where you’re looking with no camera at all — just the display itself.
Where you’d meet it: Future headsets.

WO 2026/072497 · real patent

The Screen Is the Sensor

What it does: Weaves tiny light-sensors into the screen so the display can watch your eyes from inside itself.
Where you’d meet it: Future screens.

WO 2016/097470 · real patent

Blurry Backgrounds Before They Were Cool

What it does: Teamed a main camera with helper cameras to blur photo backgrounds — two years before Portrait mode existed.
Where you’d meet it: Every phone camera today.

US 10,089,740 · real patent

Cleaning Up 3D Vision

What it does: Math that scrubs the noise out of 3D depth maps from light-field cameras.
Where you’d meet it: 3D cameras.

US 9,898,856 · real patent

No More Stretched Faces

What it does: Uses depth to un-stretch faces at the edges of wide-angle selfies. His most-cited invention!
Where you’d meet it: Your phone’s front camera.

US 9,170,580 · real patent

Robot Choreography

What it does: Math for planning the smoothest possible moves for robots with extra joints.
Where you’d meet it: Robots.

US 2012/0068927 · real patent

The Twisty Mouse

What it does: A computer mouse that can sense twisting, not just sliding — his earliest invention on record.
Where you’d meet it: Computer input devices.
